# Design Assets

All committed mockups and GIFs are manually rendered design illustrations, not evidence of a running app or real server connection. Use the current [Product Plan](../docs/developer/guides/product-plan.md),[Development Plan](../docs/developer/guides/development-plan.md) and [Desktop Interaction](../docs/developer/architecture/desktop-interaction.md) for behavior and budgets.

The original assets and their source/provenance are preserved in the [historical snapshot](../docs/developer/architecture/history/README.md). v5 sidebar/menu proposals are superseded by the v6 internal desktop. GIF timing is illustration only, not product performance. Existing render source/scene files retain their historical language; do not modify approved evidence to make a new claim.

The [system architecture](../docs/developer/architecture/system-architecture.md) is the maintained process diagram. Implementation/target evidence belongs to [Release Readiness](../docs/developer/guides/release-readiness.md).
